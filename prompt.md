## Target audience

The gym targets:
- Men and women aged roughly 18–45
- Beginners who want structured training
- Intermediate lifters
- People interested in strength training
- People trying to improve general fitness
- Busy professionals who prefer high-quality facilities

The brand should feel premium and serious, but not intimidating.

## Visual direction

Create a polished American commercial gym aesthetic.

Use:
- Large bold typography
- Dark backgrounds
- Strong contrast
- Large gym photography placeholders
- Clean spacing
- Modern cards
- Subtle gradients
- Smooth hover effects
- Light animations
- Professional UI

Avoid making it look like a hardcore bodybuilding dungeon.

Suggested palette:
- Near black
- Charcoal gray
- Off-white
- One strong accent such as electric red, burnt orange, or lime

Use a clean sans-serif font such as Inter, Manrope, Geist, or similar.
